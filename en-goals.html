Objectives and plans

  • Institute profile
  • Institute director
  • History
  • Objectives and plans
  • Institute structure

Constraints on investment in electrical-network expansion make it increasingly necessary to use existing transmission capacity close to its security limits. The competitive environment created by electricity markets has intensified this need. Security constraints and the risk of blackouts nevertheless limit full use of available capacity. Maintaining the security and quality of electrical networks and their equipment is therefore essential to reducing risks and must be addressed when planning the development and operation of the national grid. A reliable electrical network requires many complex studies. Dynamic stability, protection, operation and power quality are among the most important areas. Developing domestic knowledge, techniques and methods for assessing and improving grid security and reliability is vital, especially in a competitive electricity market. Establishing a research centre focused on electrical-network problems is necessary to develop these capabilities within the country. The purpose of establishing the energy-distribution networks research institute is to contribute to studies and technology in electrical networks and address the country's research needs in this field. Four research groups cover a broad range of these needs: Network Monitoring, Security and Protection; Network Planning and Control; Telecommunications and Communication Systems; and Restructuring.
